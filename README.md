## About Alchemist

**Alchemist** is a peaceful yet engaging alchemy simulation game where players step into a mystical laboratory to experiment with elemental ingredients and brew powerful potions.

### 🧪 Gameplay Overview
- **Intuitive Crafting:** Drag & drop magical ingredients into your glowing cauldron.
- **Recipe Discovery:** Experiment with different combinations to craft over 50 unique recipes!
- **Real-time Reactions:** Enjoy satisfying particle effects and dynamic brewing animations.
- **Progression:** Fill your spellbook, discover rare Masterworks, and hone your alchemy skills.

### ⛓️ Web3 Integration
- **On-chain High Scores:** Submit your high scores and discovered potions to **Base Mainnet**.
- **ERC-8004 Trustless Agents:** Track agent reputation securely on-chain.
- **ERC-8021 Attribution:** Full on-chain builder and platform attribution.
- **Interoperability:** Connect with any Web3 wallet to interact with on-chain lab mechanics.

### 🌌 Visuals & Sound
- **Atmosphere:** Mystical, glowing "Frosted Glass" laboratory aesthetics.
- **Feedback:** Immersive interactive sounds, bubbles, and magical chimes upon successful brews.

**Play. Experiment. Master the Art of Alchemy.**
